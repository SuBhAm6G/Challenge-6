## 2. Scientific Honesty & Zero-Vaporware Guarantees

- **No Scale Substitution:** Never passes off European EAQI or US EPA values as India NAQI.
- **No Unreliable Factor Fabrication:** Leaves tailpipe $\text{PM}_{2.5}/\text{NO}_x$ as `null` (`"Omitted (varies)"`) for electric vehicles, metros, and mixed-fleet buses rather than guessing values from $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$.
- **No Silent Synthetic Fallback:** Synthetic demo scenarios (`src/providers/sample-data.ts`) are strictly opt-in via **Load Sample Demo** with an explicit amber warning banner.
- **No Exposure/Emission Conflation:** Never assumes zero-tailpipe commutes reduce personal particulate exposure during `Poor`–`Severe` smog episodes.
