# Data Sources & Provenance

All authoritative sources used by Saansathi are centrally registered in `src/data/sources.ts` and validated by `tests/unit/data/registries.test.ts`:

| Source ID             | Organization                                  | Purpose                                                                                            | Known Limitations & Disclosures                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `cpcb-naqi`           | Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), India | Official India NAQI breakpoints, 6 categories, linear interpolation, and 3-pollutant coverage rule | Official NAQI expects 24h/8h rolling station averages; hourly forecast values are disclosed as indicative estimates |
| `open-meteo-aq`       | Open-Meteo (CAMS / Copernicus)                | Hourly & forecast concentrations for PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, and O₃                             | ~11 km atmospheric model grid; does not capture street-canyon or localized burning spikes                           |
| `who-aqg-2021`        | World Health Organization                     | Scientific pollutant health context and guideline benchmarks                                       | Health-based benchmark only; not substituted for CPCB legal AQI categories                                          |
| `epa-air-cleaners`    | US EPA                                        | Portable HEPA air cleaner CADR sizing & source control principles                                  | Adapted for Indian urban context                                                                                    |
| `epa-cleaner-room`    | US EPA                                        | Designated cleaner-air room planning during high particulate events                                | Paired with Indian combustion/cooking ventilation warnings                                                          |
| `epa-ozone-caution`   | US EPA                                        | Prohibition of ozone-generating air cleaners in occupied rooms                                     | Applied as a hard safety guardrail in engine and AI filter                                                          |
| `india-ghg-transport` | WRI India / India GHG Program / TERI          | Indian passenger-km road transport CO₂e and tailpipe factors across 12 modes                       | Average fleet factors, not marginal or vehicle-specific telemetry; PM2.5/NOₓ left `null` where unreliable           |
